Bitefinder

AKA "So You Want to Bite People"

This is a filterable table with Pathfinder 2nd Edition's various unarmed attacks granted by ancestries/heritages!

A tool by YellowAfterlife, written in Haxe.

Other ways to obtain unarmed attacks

Rest assured, this isn't an exhaustive list.

Classes

Some classes (and dedications of) grant unarmed attacks:

  • Barbarians can gain 1d10 attacks through Animal Instinct while raging. These increase to 1d12 at level 7.
    However! If you can get a 1d8 unarmed attack some other way, you might be better off taking an Instinct with a higher flat damage increase instead.
  • Magus has Arcane Fists1 for 1d6 lethal fist attacks.
  • Witch has Witch Armaments1 for a few unarmed attacks, notable options being Living Hair (1d4 Bludgeoning with Disarm, Trip, Agile, Finesse) and Iron Teeth (d8 Piercing).
    If weird AP feats are allowed, hair can be later upgraded via Syu Tak-Nwa's Deadly Hair6 to 1d6 with added Grapple, thus replacing most uses of a free hand as far as athletic manuevers are concerned.
  • Monks get some stances that do 1d8 damage without requiring you to be unarmored. Crushing Grab2 is also cool if you're doing athletics.
Dedications
  • Clawdancer2 grants a stance for 1d6 Slashing / 1d8 Slashing unarmed attacks with feats for added options.
  • Rivethun Invoker2 gets a Claw4 feat with 1d8 Spirit damage with Agile, Finesse, and Magical traits.
    However, this attack doesn't inherit your weapon proficiency runes, so it scales pretty poorly.
  • Stone Brawler2 grants a stance for 1d8 Bludgeoning unarmed attacks with Forceful and Magical traits.
    However, this dedication is lock-in and only has a single feat to choose from at levels 4 and 6.
  • Dragon Disciple2 (kobolds only) has a Claw4 feat for a 1d6 Slashing claws attack with Agile and Finesse.
  • Sterling Dynamo2 (uncommon) gets a few options (1d6 Bludgeoning, 1d4 Bludgeoning) with Grapple/Trip and an option for dice increment at cost of spending 1 action to wind up the dynamo.
  • Wrestler2 doesn't give you any new attacks, but has a bunch of Athletics-related fun things to do
  • Spirit Warrior2 increases your fist die size to d6 and gives it a Parry trait, with a later option to have +2 AC from parrying with your fist.
  • Mind Smith2 allows you to create a variety of weapons (notably one-handed 1d8 and two-handed 1d10 with Reach) and then add Grapple / Trip to it.
  • Pactbound2 (rare) offers an option to get 1d8 Bludgeoning horns6 with Shove and Trip traits. And on level 16 you can become Large and get Reach...

And here are some dedications that are probably not worth taking just for an unarmed attack:

  • Vampire2 (rare) gets Fangs (1d6 Piercing with Grapple) and has a Claw6 feat for 1d4 Slashing with a free grapple if you hit the same enemy twice in one round.
  • Zombie2 (rare) gets a jaws with 1d8 Piercing, but you can only bite a creature that you grabbed.
  • Ghoul2 (rare) gets Claws (1d4 Slashing with Agile, Finesse) and Jaws (1d6 Piercing with Finesse)
  • Werecreatures2 (rare) offer a variety of unarmed attacks with some drawbacks
