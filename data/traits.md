# General
- Activate: Takes an extra action to set up, usually lasts for a round.
- Ranged #ft+: (range increment of $1ft)
- Ranged #ft: (fixed range of $1ft)

# sf2e-specific
- +@ damage@: Strikes deal additional $1 damage.
- Stupefy: Stupefy on hit with a Will save.
- Close-Range: Reach can't exceed 5ft, and takes a -2 penalty if target isn't grabbed, helpless, prone, or restrained.
